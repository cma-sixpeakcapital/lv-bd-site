Behind the number

What stands behind every line

Committed costs, not quotes

Rates come from executed subcontracts and audited final costs, reconciled from GMP through change orders to final payment. Quotes run lower than buyout; we price where trades actually land.

Your drawings drive it

Quantities, construction type, parking, HVAC system, finishes and cladding are read off the plan set and cited by sheet. Where the plans are silent, the assumption is stated, never buried.

Current market, dated

The library is weighted toward recent buyouts and refreshed against live job costs, so each estimate carries a market as-of date and reflects today's trade pricing.

The process

Four steps from plan set to number

01

Read the plans

Schedules, sections and code areas set every budget-driving input.

02

03

04

Open items go back to you to confirm before the number is final.

What you receive

More than one number

  • A full line-item budgetIn our GMP format, each line with cost per square foot and per unit.
  • A scope-confidence flag on every lineTight, Moderate or Wide, from real bid spreads, so you see where scope needs tightening.
  • A plan-cited clarifications listEach input marked Confirmed with its sheet, or Assumed with the reason.
  • Value-engineering optionsQuantified, and drawn from savings we have accepted on past jobs.

Know its limits

What a ROM from a filed plan set cannot see

A ROM is a hard-cost benchmark. It is not a guaranteed maximum price, a lump-sum bid or an offer.

  • No site visit, and no input from the owner or the design team.
  • No soils, structural, civil or building systems engineering beyond what the filed set shows.
  • Scope and quantities are our reading of the drawings and may differ from the current design.
  • It excludes land, design and other soft costs, permits and fees, utility connection charges, off-site work, furniture and equipment, and owner contingency unless listed.
  • Pricing is as of a stated date and is not held for any period.
