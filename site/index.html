How we work

Partnership, not competition.

We would rather be your builder from the first budget than one of several bids at the end. Bidding general contractors against each other can lower the number on paper and raise it in the field, through change orders and delay.

We can bid your job against other general contractors, and we will if you ask. We do not think it is in your best interest.

Seven steps, from first call to keys
Select a step to read it.
1Step

Partnership alignment

We start with a plain conversation about your goals, capital structure and timeline. If there is a fit, we come on as your builder from the first budget. Preconstruction, estimating, project management and field supervision are in-house, so the team that budgets your building is the team that builds it.

  • Scope review
  • Timeline
  • Team fit
2Step

Preconstruction and budgeting

You get a line-item estimate priced from our library of bought-out subcontract costs, at no cost, so you can settle your underwriting before the equity and debt conversations. As the drawings develop, we update the estimate from the new plans.

  • Line-item estimate
  • Our own cost data
  • Underwriting support
3Step

Project capitalization

With Six Peak Capital, we advise on capitalization and, in select circumstances, provide credit enhancement and capital for projects. With equity and debt committed, the hard bid is timed to the construction loan closing.

  • Capital advisory
  • Credit enhancement
  • Loan coordination
4Step

Subcontractor bidding

With a closing and a start date in clear sight, scoped bid packages go out to our trade base. We level the bids scope for scope, and you see every number we see. That is where competition lowers cost without adding risk. Full bidding takes seven to nine weeks.

  • Scope-for-scope leveling
  • Open book
  • Seven to nine weeks
5Step

Guaranteed maximum price

With hard bids in hand and a verified start date, LV commits to a guaranteed maximum price. It rests on subcontractor bids, not estimates, which gives your lender and investor the certainty to close.

  • Hard bids in hand
  • Verified start date
  • Lender ready
6Step

Mobilization

With contracts signed and buyout under way, we mobilize. Established trade relationships mean a fast buyout, clean contracts and a site team ready to break ground.

  • Contracts
  • Buyout
  • Site ready
7Step

Construction and closeout

From groundbreaking to certificate of occupancy, we track cost and schedule as the job runs and report monthly, so the owner, the lender and the investor see the same picture. Closeout ends with a clean turnover.

  • Cost tracking
  • Monthly reporting
  • Clean turnover
