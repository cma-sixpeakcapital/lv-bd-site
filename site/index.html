How we work

Partnership, not competition.

We would rather be your builder from the first budget than one of several bids at the end. Bidding general contractors against each other can lower the number on paper and raise it in the field, through change orders and delay.

We can bid your job against other general contractors, and we will if you ask. We do not think it is in your best interest.

One team from the first budget

Preconstruction, estimating, project management and field supervision are in-house. The team that budgets your building is the team that builds it.

Competition among the trades

Every trade is bid to the market, and you see the bids. That is where competition lowers cost without adding risk.

Capital when the deal needs it

With Six Peak Capital, we advise on capitalization and, in select circumstances, provide credit enhancement and capital for projects.

What we build

Multifamily, mixed-use, commercial and affordable.

Multifamily

Wood-frame, podium and mid-rise apartments, from 11 to 232 units.

Mixed-use

Ground-floor retail with apartments above, including complex podium structures.

Commercial

Ground-up commercial, drawing on our podium and concrete experience.

Affordable and tax credit

Tax credit housing through LV Affordable Construction LP, built for CASp compliance and the investor reporting that tax credit deals require.
