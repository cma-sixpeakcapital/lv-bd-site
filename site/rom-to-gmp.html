ROM to GMP

When does a construction number become a guarantee?

A project is priced again and again on the way to construction. Only the last number is guaranteed.

Four stages

Four stages. One guarantee.

  1. 01
    ROM
    Rough order of magnitude

    Built from early plans and our own bid history.

    Use it to underwrite the deal. It is a budget, not a price.

    Guaranteed?
    No
  2. 02
    Preconstruction updates

    As the drawings develop, we update the estimate from the new plans and the range narrows.

    Where it helps, we price larger or less certain trades with subcontractors early. It is still a budget.

    Guaranteed?
    No
  3. 03
    Hard bid

    With a closing and a start date in clear sight, the job goes to subcontractors for hard bids.

    Real numbers from real trades, good for a limited time.

    Guaranteed?
    Not yet
  4. 04
    GMP
    Guaranteed maximum price

    Hard bids in hand and a verified start date: now LV commits.

    You pay the cost of the work plus our fee, capped at the GMP for the agreed scope.

    Guaranteed?
    Yes
Why the start date matters

The guarantee is tied to the day you start.

Subcontractor bids are only good for a limited window, so we take hard bids when the closing and the start date are real. If the start then slips past the window, the trades re-price and the guarantee has to be reset.

Start inside the window
Bid window
The trades hold their prices. The guarantee stands.
Start after the window
Bid window
The trades re-price. The guarantee has to be reset.
A ROM is a budget. A GMP is a guarantee. The guarantee takes hard bids and a verified start date.
Questions

What developers ask us

What does a ROM cost?

Nothing. It is free, and it is not a bid.

What do you need from me?

The set you have, at whatever stage it is. A link to the drawings is enough.

How long is a ROM good for?

Each estimate carries a market as-of date. It is not held for any period.

What does the GMP cover?

The cost of the work plus our fee, capped at the guaranteed maximum for the agreed scope. A change you direct moves the GMP by change order.

Do you hard bid?

Yes. With a closing and a start date in sight, we bid every trade to the market and open the book to you. Until then, the number is a budget.

Will you bid against other general contractors?

We can, and we will if you ask. We do not think it is in your best interest: it can lower the number on paper and raise it in the field. We would rather open the book and put the competition among the trades.
